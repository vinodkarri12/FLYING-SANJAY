## 🎮 Gameplay & Physics
- **Anti-Gravity Mechanic:** Tap the screen, click, or press <kbd>Space</kbd> to reverse gravity between ceiling and floor.
- **Player Character:** Sanjay's face cut out with a glowing blue border, flying with a superhero red cape, dynamic face wobble animation, and full 180° somersault flips.
- **Reel Atmosphere:** Instagram-style side actions (❤️ 1.4M likes, 💬 52.8K comments, 🚀 Share, 🎵 DSP // Mass Dance Beats).
- **Celebratory Confetti:** Golden celebratory confetti bursts upon beating your high score!
